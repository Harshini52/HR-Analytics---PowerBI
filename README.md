 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

 📊 Project Overview

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition and workforce patterns.

The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, education, salary, job roles, age groups, and years of experience to help understand employee attrition.

 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can impact workforce stability and organizational performance.

This project analyzes HR data to answer questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which age groups have higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary by gender?
- Which education fields have higher attrition?
- Which salary slabs experience higher attrition?
- Which job roles have higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary with years at the company?

 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Employees: 1,470
- Total Attrition: 238
- Attrition Rate: 16.2%
- Average Age: 37
- Average Salary: 6.5K
- Average Years at Company: 7.0

 📈 Dashboard Analysis

 1. Attrition by Gender
Analyzed employee attrition distribution across male and female employees.

 2. Attrition by Education
Compared attrition across different education fields such as Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical, and others.

 3. Attrition by Age
Analyzed attrition across different employee age groups.

 4. Attrition by Salary Slab
Examined attrition across different salary ranges.

 5. Attrition by Years at Company
Analyzed how employee attrition varies according to employee tenure.

 6. Attrition by Job Role
Compared attrition across different job roles to identify roles with higher employee turnover.
